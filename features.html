Protocol Features

Everything Different

SIKKA rethinks every layer of a digital currency — from the ledger structure to the cryptography to the network transport.

Always
Zero Fees — Forever
When you send SIKKA, the exact amount arrives in full. There are no gas fees, no priority fees, no miner tips, no protocol cuts. Zero. The recipient receives exactly what you sent — always.
$0.00
Per transaction, any amount, forever
No Transaction History
SIKKA keeps balances, not a public ledger of every payment. Transactions are batched into checkpoints, consensus signs the state root, and the transactions are then discarded. Past payments are not reconstructable — private by default.
0
Public tx logs

State proof
Checkpoint Consensus
Round-robin proposers batch transactions into a checkpoint. Validators vote and a checkpoint finalizes once ≥2/3 of the bonded stake agrees.
Atomic Account Balances
Transfers debit the sender and credit the recipient atomically in CHILLAR integer arithmetic. No gas, no change outputs, no rounding — the recipient gets exactly what you send.
Account-Based, Pure-Rust
Balances live in a tiny ACID key-value store on top of redb. Three fixed tables, zero C/C++ dependencies. Storage grows with accounts, not with every transfer.
Spam Resistance

Per-Account Spend Credits

No useless proof-of-work. Instead every account earns spend credits that regenerate over time — enough for daily use, too slow for abuse.

Security

Post-Quantum by Design

No classical elliptic curve fallbacks. Every key, every signature, every hash uses algorithms hardened against quantum computers.

ML-DSA-87
NIST FIPS 204 Post-Quantum
SIKKA uses Module-Lattice-Based Digital Signature Algorithm at the highest security level (87 = NIST Level 5). This is the strongest post-quantum signature standard published by NIST. There are no classical ECDSA or Ed25519 fallbacks — every signature in SIKKA is quantum-resistant by default.
