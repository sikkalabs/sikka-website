SIKKA Token

A 1:1 Bridge to the Future

Buy SIKKA on Ethereum today, bridge natively to the Sikka chain when you're ready. Same value, same chain — just a door in.

Pegged 1:1 with Native SIKKA
Every SIKKA token on Ethereum is backed one-for-one by native SIKKA held in escrow on the Sikka blockchain. No double-spend, no diluted value — 1 token is 1 SIKKA, always.
Bridge Natively, Directly to the Chain
The bridge is a one-way burn: name your 32-byte Sikka address, burn your Ethereum SIKKA, and a relayer delivers native SIKKA to that address from escrow. No wrapping, no minting, no hop.
Open the bridge
Escrowed, Fixed Supply
Total supply is fixed at 19,960,907 SIKKA — the same genesis supply as the mainnet. The Ethereum token is the guest pass, not the chain.
Freedom While the Network Matures
While the chain matures, SIKKA on Ethereum gives you freedom — trade on Uniswap, Matcha, DexScreener and the rest of the ecosystem, then bridge in whenever you're ready.
