How the bridge works

The SIKKA token is a guest pass on Ethereum. Its fixed supply of 19,960,907 SIKKA is fully escrowed on the Sikka blockchain. The only way out is one-way: burn the token naming your Sikka address, and a relayer delivers native SIKKA from escrow to that address.

1. Connect your wallet on Ethereum.
2. Enter the amount to burn.
3. Enter your 32-byte Sikka address.
4. Confirm the burn — a relayer does the rest.

From Ethereum to the Chain

Staking, zero-fee micropayments, and everything else only exist on the Sikka chain. The burn is how you get there.

1

Get SIKKA on Ethereum

Buy the SIKKA token on Uniswap or any major DEX. The token is pegged 1:1 with native SIKKA — the supply is fully escrowed on the Sikka chain.

Uniswap
2

Get your Sikka address

Generate a 32-byte ML-DSA-87 keypair in the web wallet or your node. Your Sikka address is the 64-hex public key where native SIKKA will be delivered.

Open the web wallet
3

Burn & bridge

Enter the amount and your Sikka address above, then confirm the burn. A relayer watches the Burned event and delivers native SIKKA from escrow to your address — 1:1, directly on the chain.
